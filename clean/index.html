Services

What we do

Four kinds of work, all for homes. If your project is a mix of them, that's normal. One call covers it.

  • Exterior painting

    Walls, trim, front doors and garage doors. We patch and prime first so the new color goes on clean.

    • Full exterior repaints
    • Trim, doors and garage doors
    • Color changes

  • Stucco

    Cracks, stains and worn patches repaired and refinished, then painted so it blends with the rest of the wall.

    • Crack and patch repair
    • Refinishing worn walls
    • Paint to match

  • Flooring

    Old floor out, subfloor prepped, new floor in. We install vinyl plank, laminate and stair treads.

    • Luxury vinyl plank (LVP)
    • Laminate
    • Stair treads

  • Handyman and repairs

    Installations and small repairs for your house or apartment. Send us the list and we'll tell you what we can take on.

    • Small installations
    • Repairs around the home
    • Not sure? Just ask

How it works

Four steps, no mystery

  1. 1

    Estimate

    Call, text or send the form. We come take a look and give you a price.

  2. 2

    Prep

    We cover and protect what stays, then clean, patch and repair so the surface is ready.

  3. 3

    Paint or install

    The color goes on, or the floor goes down. We keep you posted as we go.

  4. 4

    Walkthrough

    We walk the job with you at the end. If something's off, we fix it before we call it done.
